# English Grammar: 01 (MCQ and Written)

---

## 1. Parts of Speech

Noun · Pronoun · Verb · Adjective · Adverb · Preposition · Conjunction · Interjection

**Tip:** A word's part of speech depends on its use in the sentence.

- _He runs fast._ (fast = adverb) · _It is a fast car._ (fast = adjective)

---

## 2. Articles

**A / An** (by sound, not spelling)

- _an_ before vowel sounds: an hour, an honest man, an MP, an umbrella
- _a_ before consonant sounds: a university, a European, a one-taka note, a useful book

**The** is used with:

- Unique things: the sun, the moon, the sky
- Superlatives: the best, the tallest
- Rivers, seas, oceans, mountain ranges, island groups: the Padma, the Bay of Bengal, the Himalayas
- Newspapers, holy books, ships: The Daily Star, the Quran, the Titanic
- Nationalities as a group: the English, the Bangladeshis
- Specific or already-mentioned things

**No article:**

- Languages, meals, games, subjects: _She speaks Bangla. We had lunch. He plays cricket._
- Proper nouns, abstract nouns used generally
- Fixed phrases: go to school / bed / hospital / prison (for their normal purpose)

## 3. Tenses (Structure)

| Tense       | Simple          | Continuous        | Perfect        | Perfect Continuous     |
| ----------- | --------------- | ----------------- | -------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Present** | V1 (s/es)       | am/is/are + V-ing | has/have + V3  | has/have been + V-ing  |
| **Past**    | V2              | was/were + V-ing  | had + V3       | had been + V-ing       |
| **Future**  | will/shall + V1 | will be + V-ing   | will have + V3 | will have been + V-ing |

**Key rules**

- Present Perfect with _already, yet, just, ever, never, so far, since, for_: _I have lived here for ten years._
- Past Simple with _yesterday, ago, last year, in 2010_: _He came yesterday._ (not "has come yesterday")
- _Since_ + point in time; _for_ + duration
- After _if / when / as soon as / before / after_ (time/condition clause), use present for future: _If it rains, we will stay._
- Universal truths stay in present: _The teacher said that the earth moves round the sun._

---

## 4. Subject-Verb Agreement

1. **Each, every, either, neither, one, everyone, anybody** + singular verb: _Each of the boys is present._
2. **One of the + plural noun** + singular verb: _One of my friends is a doctor._
3. **The number of** + singular verb; **A number of** + plural verb.
4. **As well as, with, along with, together with, in addition to**: verb agrees with the **first** subject. _The teacher, along with his students, was present._
5. **Either...or / Neither...nor / Not only...but also**: verb agrees with the **nearer** subject. _Neither he nor they were present._
6. **Many a** + singular noun + singular verb: _Many a man has failed._
7. Singular-looking nouns: news, mathematics, physics, economics, politics, measles → singular verb.
8. Plural nouns: police, people, cattle, gentry → plural verb.
9. Amounts, distances, time as one unit → singular: _Ten miles is a long way._
10. **The + adjective** (the poor, the rich) → plural verb.
11. Title of a book/film → singular: _"Gulliver's Travels" is a famous book._

## 6. Voice Change (Active → Passive)

| Tense              | Active                    | Passive                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Present simple     | She writes a letter.      | A letter is written by her.        |
| Present continuous | She is writing a letter.  | A letter is being written by her.  |
| Present perfect    | She has written a letter. | A letter has been written by her.  |
| Past simple        | She wrote a letter.       | A letter was written by her.       |
| Past continuous    | She was writing a letter. | A letter was being written by her. |
| Past perfect       | She had written a letter. | A letter had been written by her.  |
| Future simple      | She will write a letter.  | A letter will be written by her.   |
| Modals             | She can write a letter.   | A letter can be written by her.    |

**Special cases**

- Imperative: _Open the door._ → _Let the door be opened._ / _You are requested to open the door._
- Interrogative: _Who wrote the book?_ → _By whom was the book written?_
- Two objects: _He gave me a book._ → _I was given a book by him._ / _A book was given to me by him._

## 7. Narration (Direct → Indirect)

**Tense shift (when reporting verb is past)**

| Direct             | Indirect              |
| ------------------ | --------------------- |
| Present simple     | Past simple           |
| Present continuous | Past continuous       |
| Present perfect    | Past perfect          |
| Past simple        | Past perfect          |
| will / can / may   | would / could / might |

**Word changes:** now → then · today → that day · tomorrow → the next day · yesterday → the previous day · here → there · this → that · these → those · ago → before · come → go

**By sentence type**

- Assertive: _said that..._ → _He said, "I am happy."_ → _He said that he was happy._
- Interrogative (yes/no): _asked if/whether..._ → _He said, "Are you ready?"_ → _He asked me if I was ready._
- Interrogative (wh-): keep the wh-word, use statement word order.
- Imperative: _ordered/requested/advised + to-infinitive_ → _He said to me, "Go home."_ → _He ordered me to go home._
- Exclamatory: _exclaimed with joy/sorrow that..._
- Optative: _prayed/wished that..._ → _He said, "May you be happy."_ → _He prayed that I might be happy._

**No tense change** if the statement is a universal truth or habitual fact.

## 8. Right Form of Verbs

**Gerund (V-ing) after:** enjoy, avoid, finish, mind, suggest, practise, deny, admit, postpone, delay, give up, keep, cannot help, look forward to, object to, be used to, be accustomed to, it is no use, worth

- _I am looking forward to meeting you._

**To-infinitive after:** want, hope, decide, plan, promise, refuse, manage, afford, learn, agree, fail, expect, need, wish

- _She decided to leave._

**Bare infinitive (V1) after:** modals (can, will, must...), _let, make, see, hear, help_, _had better, would rather, why not_

- _He made me laugh. You had better go._

**Conditionals**

| Type | If-clause                    | Main clause                                                 |
| ---- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Zero | present                      | present (facts): _If you heat ice, it melts._               |
| 1st  | present                      | will + V1: _If it rains, we will stay._                     |
| 2nd  | past (were for all subjects) | would + V1: _If I were you, I would go._                    |
| 3rd  | past perfect                 | would have + V3: _If he had studied, he would have passed._ |

**Wish / as if / it is time**

- _I wish I were a bird._ (present unreal)
- _I wish I had studied._ (past unreal)
- _He talks as if he knew everything._
- _It is high time we left._ (past form, present meaning)
- _Lest ... should_: _Work hard lest you should fail._
- _Would rather ... than_: _I would rather walk than take a bus._

## 10. Transformation of Sentences

**Degree**

- Positive: _No other city in Bangladesh is as large as Dhaka._
- Comparative: _Dhaka is larger than any other city in Bangladesh._
- Superlative: _Dhaka is the largest city in Bangladesh._

**Affirmative ↔ Negative**

- _He is rich._ → _He is not poor._
- _Everyone loves him._ → _No one hates him._ / _There is none who does not love him._

**Assertive ↔ Interrogative ↔ Exclamatory**

- _It is a very hot day._ → _What a hot day it is!_ → _Isn't it a very hot day?_

**Simple ↔ Complex ↔ Compound**

- Simple: _Seeing the tiger, he ran away._
- Complex: _When he saw the tiger, he ran away._
- Compound: _He saw the tiger and ran away._

**Too ... to / So ... that**

- _He is too weak to walk._ → _He is so weak that he cannot walk._

## 11. Common Errors (Correction)

1. ✗ more better, most best → ✓ better, best (no double comparative)
2. ✗ senior than me → ✓ senior **to** me
3. ✗ I have seen him yesterday → ✓ I **saw** him yesterday
4. ✗ Each of the boys have → ✓ Each of the boys **has**
5. ✗ informations, advices, furnitures, luggages, knowledges → ✓ **uncountable**: information, advice, furniture, luggage, knowledge, news, equipment, evidence, scenery, hair
6. ✗ fewer water → ✓ **less** water; **fewer** books (countable)
7. ✗ much books → ✓ **many** books
8. ✗ He is knowing → ✓ He **knows** (stative verbs: know, like, love, see, believe, want, own, belong are not used in continuous)
9. ✗ Hardly had he come than... → ✓ Hardly had he come **when/before**...; No sooner had he come **than**...
10. ✗ The reason is because → ✓ The reason is **that**
11. ✗ Who did you meet? (formal) → ✓ **Whom** did you meet?
12. ✗ lay/lie confusion: _lie, lay, lain_ (rest) vs _lay, laid, laid_ (put); _rise, rose, risen_ (intransitive) vs _raise, raised, raised_ (transitive)
13. ✗ He is one of the best player → ✓ one of the best **players**
14. ✗ I prefer tea than coffee → ✓ I prefer tea **to** coffee
15. ✗ Not only he but also his brother were → ✓ Not only he but also his brother **was** (nearer subject)
16. Its (possessive) vs It's (it is)
17. Few = almost none; a few = some; the few = all there are. Little = almost none; a little = some.
18. Parallelism: ✗ He likes swimming and to run → ✓ He likes **swimming and running**

## 13. Written Part

**Application format**

1. Date (top right)
2. To: The [designation], [office/organisation], [address]
3. Subject: (short, specific)
4. Salutation: Sir / Madam / Dear Sir
5. Body: introduce yourself, state the problem/request, give reasons, end politely
6. Closing: Yours faithfully / Yours obediently, name, address

**Paragraph/Composition**

- Topic sentence → 3 to 5 supporting sentences → concluding sentence
- Common BCS topics: Digital Bangladesh, Corruption, Climate Change, Load-shedding, Mobile Phone, Our National Flag, Padma Bridge, Role of Youth, A Rainy Day, Importance of Education

**Translation (Bangla ↔ English)**

- Fix the tense first, then the subject, then the verb form.
- Read the full Bangla sentence before translating. Do not translate word by word.
- Keep sentences simple; correct simple sentences score better than wrong complex ones.

**Amplification:** Expand a quotation into one paragraph: explain the meaning → give reasons → add an example → conclude.

## 14. Practice MCQs (Answers in Bold)

1. He is senior \_\_\_ me. → a) than b) **to** c) from d) of
2. Neither of the boys \_\_\_ present. → a) were b) are c) **was** d) have been
3. I look forward to \_\_\_ you. → a) see b) saw c) **seeing** d) be seen
4. If I \_\_\_ you, I would accept the offer. → a) am b) **were** c) was d) will be
5. The number of students \_\_\_ increasing. → a) are b) **is** c) were d) have
6. Passive of "She is writing a letter": a) A letter is written by her b) **A letter is being written by her** c) A letter was written by her d) A letter has been written by her
7. Indirect of _He said, "I am happy."_: a) He said that I am happy b) **He said that he was happy** c) He said he is happy d) He said that he had been happy
8. One who hates mankind: a) misogynist b) **misanthrope** c) philanthropist d) pessimist
9. "A white elephant" means: a) a rare animal b) **a costly but useless possession** c) a lucky charm d) a huge gift
10. He is accustomed \_\_\_ early rising. → a) with b) for c) **to** d) at
